## Authored examples

### convincing — giving strong reason to believe

Making something seem true or reasonable through clear reasons or evidence.

có sức thuyết phục

Her explanation of the missing payment was convincing.

Lời giải thích của cô ấy về khoản thanh toán bị thiếu có sức thuyết phục.

Used before a noun or after a linking verb; describes the strength of persuasion, not proof that a claim is true.

### exercise — activity for fitness

Physical activity done to improve or maintain health and fitness.

việc tập thể dục

A short walk gives me some exercise during my lunch break.

Một cuộc đi bộ ngắn giúp tôi vận động trong giờ nghỉ trưa.

Usually uncountable for physical activity in general; an exercise can name a particular movement.

### exercise — do physical activity

To move your body as an activity intended to improve fitness.

tập thể dục

They exercise together in the park before breakfast.

Họ cùng tập thể dục trong công viên trước bữa sáng.

Intransitive in this sense; exercise a muscle is a related transitive construction.

### exercise — task for practising

A task or activity designed to practise a skill or check understanding.

bài tập

The final exercise asks students to compare two short texts.

Bài tập cuối yêu cầu học sinh so sánh hai đoạn văn ngắn.

Countable; an exercise in writing or a grammar exercise specifies the skill being practised.

### exercise — use a right or ability

To put a right, power, or ability into use.

thực hiện quyền; vận dụng khả năng

Residents may exercise their right to question the proposal.

Cư dân có thể thực hiện quyền chất vấn đề xuất của mình.

Transitive; takes objects such as a right, authority, or judgment, rather than a person being trained.

### law — rule established by authority

A rule established by a governing authority that people are required to follow.

luật; quy định pháp luật

The new law requires clearer labels on food packages.

Luật mới yêu cầu nhãn rõ ràng hơn trên bao bì thực phẩm.

Countable for a particular rule; uncountable for the legal system, as in obey the law.

### law — study and profession of legal rules

The study or professional practice of legal rules and their application.

ngành luật

After studying law, Linh joined a small legal practice.

Sau khi học luật, Linh vào làm tại một văn phòng luật nhỏ.

Uncountable when naming the academic field; study law does not take an indefinite article.

### participation — involvement in an activity

The act of taking part in an activity with other people.

sự tham gia

Participation in the workshop is open to all staff.

Tất cả nhân viên đều có thể tham gia hội thảo.

Uncountable; participation in names the activity, while participation by names the participants.

### shelter — place offering protection

A place that protects people or animals from danger or bad weather.

nơi trú ẩn

The hikers found shelter in an empty barn during the storm.

Những người đi bộ đường dài tìm được nơi trú trong một nhà kho trống khi bão đến.

Uncountable for protection in general; a shelter can be a particular building or structure.

### shelter — protect from harm or weather

To give someone or something protection from a danger or harsh conditions.

che chở; bảo vệ

The high wall shelters the garden from strong winds.

Bức tường cao che chắn khu vườn khỏi gió mạnh.

Transitive shelter someone or something from; also intransitive when people shelter in a place.

### willingness — readiness to do something

The state of being ready to do something without needing to be forced.

sự sẵn lòng

Her willingness to listen helped us settle the disagreement.

Sự sẵn lòng lắng nghe của cô ấy giúp chúng tôi giải quyết bất đồng.

Uncountable; willingness to is followed by an infinitive naming the action.

### commentator — person describing and discussing events

A person who describes or discusses events for an audience, often during a broadcast.

bình luận viên

The commentator explained why the referee stopped the match.

Bình luận viên giải thích vì sao trọng tài dừng trận đấu.

Countable; a commentator on a subject may discuss it rather than describe a live event.

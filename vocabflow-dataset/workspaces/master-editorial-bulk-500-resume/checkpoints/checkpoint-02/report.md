## Real bilingual previews

### tongue — movable part inside the mouth

The soft movable part inside the mouth used in tasting, moving food, and forming speech.

lưỡi

She touched the loose tooth with her tongue.

Cô ấy dùng lưỡi chạm vào chiếc răng lung lay.

Countable; the tongue can name the organ generally, while my tongue names a person's own.

### tongue — language used by a community

A language, especially one associated with a person's home or community.

tiếng; ngôn ngữ

His grandmother tells stories in her native tongue.

Bà của anh ấy kể chuyện bằng tiếng mẹ đẻ.

Countable; native tongue and mother tongue are established expressions. This language use is more literary than language alone.

### web — network made by a spider

A structure of fine threads made by a spider, often used to catch insects.

mạng nhện

A web stretched between the two branches above the path.

Một mạng nhện giăng giữa hai cành cây phía trên lối đi.

Countable; a spider's web names the structure, not the spider itself.

### web — linked pages accessed through the internet

The system of connected pages and resources that people access through the internet.

mạng web

The museum put its opening hours on the web.

Bảo tàng đăng giờ mở cửa lên mạng web.

Usually the web in this sense; on the web describes online availability. Not every internet service is part of the web.

### chew — break food with the teeth

To work food between your teeth so that it becomes easier to swallow.

nhai

He chewed the bread slowly while listening to the story.

Anh ấy nhai bánh mì chậm rãi trong lúc nghe câu chuyện.

Transitive chew food or intransitive chew slowly; chew on can also name what is being bitten.

### habitual — done regularly as a habit

Done or experienced repeatedly as part of an established habit.

thường lệ; thành thói quen

Her habitual early arrival gave her time to unlock the hall.

Thói quen đến sớm thường lệ giúp cô ấy có thời gian mở khóa hội trường.

Usually before a noun such as behaviour or lateness; repetition does not itself imply addiction.

### motorcycle — two-wheeled motor vehicle

A road vehicle with two wheels that is powered by a motor.

xe máy; mô tô

They parked the motorcycle beside the repair shop.

Họ đỗ xe máy cạnh tiệm sửa chữa.

Countable; ride a motorcycle describes operating or travelling on it, rather than drive a motorcycle in ordinary usage.

### pleasant — giving comfort or enjoyment

Giving a feeling of enjoyment, comfort, or satisfaction.

dễ chịu; thú vị

A pleasant breeze cooled the room after lunch.

Một làn gió dễ chịu làm căn phòng mát hơn sau bữa trưa.

Before a noun or after a linking verb; pleasant describes something enjoyable rather than someone experiencing pleasure.

### site — place used for an activity

A place where something stands, happens, or is planned to be built.

địa điểm; khu đất

The old factory site will become a public garden.

Khu đất của nhà máy cũ sẽ trở thành một khu vườn công cộng.

Countable; site of an event or site for a building specifies the use or association.

### site — collection of linked online pages

A website or collection of related pages available online.

trang web; website

The charity's site lists places where volunteers are needed.

Trang web của tổ chức từ thiện liệt kê những nơi cần tình nguyện viên.

Countable; visit a site describes online access here, not physical travel.

### worldwide — extending across the world

Extending to many parts of the world.

trên toàn thế giới

The photograph attracted worldwide attention.

Bức ảnh thu hút sự chú ý trên toàn thế giới.

Adjective before a noun; worldwide attention describes the geographical reach of interest.

### worldwide — throughout the world

In or to places across the world.

trên khắp thế giới

The company sends its catalogue to readers worldwide.

Công ty gửi danh mục sản phẩm cho độc giả trên khắp thế giới.

Adverb modifying distribution or activity; it does not literally require every country or individual.

### crowd — people gathered in one place

A group of people gathered closely together in a place.

đám đông

A crowd gathered outside the station to welcome the musicians.

Một đám đông tụ tập ngoài nhà ga để chào đón các nhạc công.

Countable collective noun; a crowd of people specifies its members.

### further — additional to what already exists

More or additional beyond what has already been given or done.

thêm; bổ sung

Please contact the office for further details.

Hãy liên hệ văn phòng để biết thêm chi tiết.

Usually before a noun; further information is uncountable, while further questions is plural.

### further — at a greater distance

At or to a distance beyond the place already reached or mentioned.

xa hơn

We walked further along the bank to find a shaded spot.

Chúng tôi đi xa hơn dọc bờ để tìm một chỗ có bóng mát.

Adverb with movement or position; this draft concerns distance rather than an additional quantity.

### whose — asking who owns something

Used before a noun to ask which person owns or is associated with something.

của ai

Whose bag is beside the door?

Chiếc túi cạnh cửa là của ai?

Question determiner before a noun; whose is a possession word, while who's means who is or who has.

### whose — asking the owner without repeating the noun

Used to ask who owns something when the thing is already understood.

của ai

Several coats are here; whose is this?

Ở đây có vài chiếc áo khoác; chiếc này là của ai?

Standalone question pronoun; no noun follows whose in this construction.

### whose — linking a person or thing to a possession

Used to connect a person or thing with something belonging or related to it.

có; mà của người đó hoặc vật đó

We met a writer whose stories are set by the sea.

Chúng tôi gặp một nhà văn có những câu chuyện lấy bối cảnh bên biển.

Relative determiner before a noun; can refer to people or things and does not require an apostrophe.

### gene — unit of inherited biological information

A unit of inherited information carried in DNA that helps direct biological functions and characteristics.

gen

The lesson explained how a gene can affect the production of a protein.

Bài học giải thích cách một gen có thể ảnh hưởng đến việc tạo ra một protein.

Countable; a gene may influence a characteristic, but many characteristics depend on multiple genes and other factors.

### racist — showing prejudice or unfair treatment based on race

Showing prejudice or unfair treatment towards people because of their race or perceived racial identity.

phân biệt chủng tộc

The school removed a racist slogan from the noticeboard.

Nhà trường gỡ bỏ một khẩu hiệu phân biệt chủng tộc khỏi bảng thông báo.

Can describe language, beliefs, actions, or practices; refers to discrimination, not merely mentioning race.

### racist — person expressing racial prejudice

A person who holds or expresses prejudice against people because of their race.

người có tư tưởng hoặc hành vi phân biệt chủng tộc

The speaker challenged a racist who had insulted the visitors.

Diễn giả phản bác một người phân biệt chủng tộc đã xúc phạm các vị khách.

Countable; an evaluative term about beliefs or behaviour, not a person's racial identity.

### grapefruit — large tart citrus fruit

A large round citrus fruit with juicy flesh that often tastes tart and slightly bitter.

bưởi chùm

He cut a grapefruit in half and placed it in a small bowl.

Anh ấy bổ đôi một quả bưởi chùm rồi đặt vào một chiếc bát nhỏ.

Countable for a whole fruit; uncountable for its flesh as food. Grapefruit is distinct from the larger pomelo.

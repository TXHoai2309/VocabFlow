## Real bilingual previews

### intense — very strong in degree

Very strong in feeling, force, or degree.

mãnh liệt; dữ dội; căng thẳng

The final round required intense concentration from every player.

Vòng cuối đòi hỏi mọi người chơi phải tập trung cao độ.

Used before nouns or after linking verbs; intense describes strength, while intensive usually describes concentrated effort or activity.

### privilege — special right or advantage

A right or advantage available to a particular person or group rather than to everyone.

đặc quyền; quyền lợi đặc biệt

Access to the workshop after hours is a privilege reserved for trained members.

Quyền vào xưởng ngoài giờ là đặc quyền dành cho các thành viên đã được đào tạo.

Countable for a particular right; the privilege of doing something names the activity allowed.

### privilege — opportunity regarded as an honour

An opportunity that someone feels honoured or fortunate to have.

vinh dự; cơ hội đáng trân trọng

It was a privilege to hear the poet read her earliest work.

Được nghe nhà thơ đọc tác phẩm đầu tay của bà là một vinh dự.

Often it is a privilege to do something; this expresses appreciation rather than an exclusive legal right.

### submit — send for official consideration

To give a document or other material to someone so that it can be considered or assessed.

nộp; gửi để xét duyệt

Each designer submitted a sketch to the selection panel.

Mỗi nhà thiết kế nộp một bản phác thảo cho hội đồng tuyển chọn.

Transitive submit something to someone; submitted doubles the final t.

### submit — accept another's authority

To accept someone else's control or a requirement instead of continuing to resist it.

phục tùng; chịu khuất phục

The rebel leaders refused to submit to the governor's authority.

Các thủ lĩnh nổi dậy từ chối phục tùng quyền lực của thống đốc.

Intransitive submit to someone or something in this sense; distinct from submitting a document.

### workout — session of physical exercise

A period of physical exercise intended to practise or improve fitness.

buổi tập thể dục; buổi tập luyện

After her workout, Lan packed the exercise mat away.

Sau buổi tập, Lan cất tấm thảm tập đi.

Countable; have or do a workout. The noun is one word, while the related verb phrase work out is two words.

### creative — able to produce original ideas

Using imagination to produce original ideas or things.

sáng tạo; có óc sáng tạo

His creative use of shadows made the small stage feel larger.

Cách sử dụng bóng đổ sáng tạo của anh khiến sân khấu nhỏ có vẻ rộng hơn.

Used before a noun or after a linking verb; creative describes a person, process, or result involving imagination.

### fuse — cord that carries ignition

A cord that burns along its length to carry ignition to an explosive charge.

ngòi nổ; dây cháy chậm

The diagram labels the fuse on an old ceremonial firework.

Sơ đồ ghi tên ngòi nổ trên một loại pháo lễ hội cổ.

Countable; this is the burning-cord sense, not the electrical safety device. A short fuse is also a separate figurative expression.

### medication — medicine used for treatment

Medicine used to treat an illness or health condition.

thuốc; thuốc điều trị

The patient brought a list of her medication to the appointment.

Người bệnh mang danh sách thuốc đang dùng tới buổi hẹn.

Usually uncountable for medicine collectively; on medication describes receiving treatment. Plural medications refers to different medicines.

### ring — jewellery worn on a finger

A small band worn around a finger as jewellery.

nhẫn

He kept his grandfather's ring in a lined wooden box.

Anh giữ chiếc nhẫn của ông mình trong một chiếc hộp gỗ có lót bên trong.

Countable; a ring on someone's finger. This boundary concerns jewellery rather than any circular arrangement.

### ring — make a bell-like sound

To make a clear sound like a bell, or cause a bell to make that sound.

reo; rung chuông

The doorbell rang twice while we were unpacking.

Chuông cửa reo hai lần khi chúng tôi đang dỡ đồ.

Intransitive a bell rings; transitive ring a bell. Past tense rang and past participle rung.

### ring — contact by telephone

To telephone someone.

gọi điện

I'll ring the repair shop before taking the bicycle there.

Tôi sẽ gọi điện cho cửa hàng sửa chữa trước khi mang xe đạp tới đó.

Chiefly British; ring someone, without to before the person. Rang and rung are irregular forms.

### ring — circular band or arrangement

A circular band or an arrangement of things in a circle.

vòng; vòng tròn

The children placed a ring of stones around the toy castle.

Bọn trẻ xếp một vòng đá quanh lâu đài đồ chơi.

Countable; a ring of something names what forms the circle. Separate from a finger ring and a ringing sound.

### sceptical — doubting a claim

Not easily convinced that a statement or idea is true or useful.

hoài nghi; chưa tin

Mai was sceptical about the plan until she saw the trial results.

Mai hoài nghi về kế hoạch cho tới khi thấy kết quả thử nghiệm.

Sceptical about or of something; skeptical is a common American spelling. Doubt does not imply refusing all evidence.

### weigh — have a particular weight

To have a particular weight.

nặng; có trọng lượng

The parcel weighs less than the empty suitcase.

Bưu kiện nhẹ hơn chiếc va-li rỗng.

Intransitive weigh an amount; normally simple forms for a present weight, as in it weighs two kilos.

### weigh — find how heavy something is

To measure the weight of a person or thing.

cân; đo trọng lượng

The cook weighed the flour before mixing the dough.

Người đầu bếp cân bột mì trước khi trộn bột làm bánh.

Transitive weigh something; weighed is the regular past form. Distinct from merely having a weight.

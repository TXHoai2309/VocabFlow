## Real bilingual previews

### starch — Plant carbohydrate

A carbohydrate that plants store and that occurs in foods such as rice and potatoes.

tinh bột

The cook stirred potato starch into the sauce to thicken it.

Người đầu bếp khuấy tinh bột khoai tây vào nước xốt để làm nó sánh hơn.

Usually uncountable; potato starch, corn starch. Starchy is the adjective.

### starch — Fabric stiffener

A preparation containing starch that makes fabric firmer when applied to it.

hồ làm cứng vải

She sprayed starch on the collar before ironing the shirt.

Cô ấy xịt hồ lên cổ áo trước khi là chiếc áo.

Usually uncountable; spray starch, apply starch to fabric.

### yes — Affirmative response

Used to express agreement, permission or a positive answer.

vâng; có; đồng ý

Yes, you may borrow the green umbrella until tomorrow.

Vâng, bạn có thể mượn chiếc ô màu xanh lá đến ngày mai.

A response word, often followed by a comma. The Vietnamese form depends on the question and relationship between speakers.

### considerably — Substantial degree

By a large enough amount to make a noticeable difference.

đáng kể; nhiều

The new shelf is considerably wider than the old one.

Chiếc kệ mới rộng hơn chiếc cũ đáng kể.

Modifies comparatives or verbs: considerably larger, vary considerably.

### handbook — Practical reference book

A book that organizes essential information or instructions about a subject for easy consultation.

sổ tay hướng dẫn; sách tra cứu thực hành

The volunteer checked the handbook for instructions on setting up the projector.

Tình nguyện viên tra sổ tay hướng dẫn để tìm cách lắp máy chiếu.

Countable; a handbook on a subject, a staff handbook. It need not be small enough to hold in one hand.

### lot — Large quantity before a noun

Used in a lot of or lots of to indicate a large quantity of something.

nhiều; rất nhiều

A lot of leaves collected beside the garden gate overnight.

Rất nhiều lá tụ lại bên cổng vườn qua đêm.

Use a lot of or lots of before a plural countable noun or an uncountable noun; the verb agrees with that noun.

### lot — Large quantity without a following noun

Used in a lot or lots to refer to a large quantity already understood from context.

nhiều; rất nhiều thứ hoặc người

Some seedlings survived the frost, but a lot died.

Một số cây con sống sót qua đợt sương giá, nhưng nhiều cây đã chết.

A lot can stand without of when the noun is understood; informal. Do not write alot.

### lot — Large degree or frequent occurrence

Used in a lot to indicate a high degree or that something happens frequently.

rất nhiều; thường xuyên

My sister cycles a lot during the summer holidays.

Chị tôi đạp xe thường xuyên trong kỳ nghỉ hè.

A lot often follows the verb; a lot better strengthens a comparative. Use the two-word spelling.

### offense — Punishable act

An act that breaks a law or an established rule.

hành vi phạm pháp; hành vi vi phạm

The fictional school treats deliberately damaging equipment as a serious offense.

Ngôi trường hư cấu coi việc cố ý làm hỏng thiết bị là một hành vi vi phạm nghiêm trọng.

Countable; commit an offense, a minor offense. Offence is the usual British spelling.

### offense — Feeling insulted

Hurt or resentment caused by something seen as insulting or disrespectful.

sự phật lòng; sự xúc phạm

His careless joke caused offense to several listeners.

Câu đùa thiếu suy nghĩ của anh ấy khiến một số người nghe phật lòng.

Usually uncountable; cause offense, take offense at a remark. The spelling offence is usual in British English.

### turbine — Fluid-driven rotary machine

A machine whose blades turn when moving water, steam or gas passes through them, producing mechanical power.

tua-bin

Water from the reservoir turns a turbine inside the power station.

Nước từ hồ chứa làm quay một tua-bin bên trong nhà máy điện.

Countable; a water turbine, a steam turbine, a wind turbine. A turbine may drive a separate generator.

### whatsoever — Emphatic absence

Used after a negative expression to emphasize that there is none of the thing mentioned.

hoàn toàn không; không chút nào

The empty envelope contained no instructions whatsoever.

Phong bì rỗng hoàn toàn không có hướng dẫn nào.

Often follows no plus a noun, or none; emphatic. It normally strengthens a negative statement.

### all — Whole quantity before a noun

Used before a noun to include every member or the entire amount of something.

tất cả; toàn bộ

All the blue folders belong on the upper shelf.

Tất cả các bìa hồ sơ màu xanh thuộc về ngăn kệ trên cùng.

All the plus noun; all of them. Use all day without the for a whole day.

### all — Whole quantity standing alone

Every person or thing in a group, or the whole amount being discussed.

tất cả mọi người; tất cả mọi thứ

The chairs were repaired, and all are ready for tomorrow's meeting.

Các ghế đã được sửa, và tất cả đều sẵn sàng cho cuộc họp ngày mai.

May stand alone when the group is understood; all of us, all of it. Verb number follows the referent.

### all — Complete degree

Used to emphasize that a state applies completely.

hoàn toàn; hết

After drying in the sun, the towel was all dry.

Sau khi phơi dưới nắng, chiếc khăn đã khô hoàn toàn.

Occurs before adjectives in expressions such as all ready or all alone; context can distinguish degree from reference to every member.

### harmonize — Musical harmony

To sing or play notes that form a harmony with a melody, or to add such notes to it.

hát hoặc chơi hòa âm; phối hòa âm

Two singers harmonized with the melody played on the piano.

Hai ca sĩ hát hòa âm với giai điệu được chơi trên đàn piano.

Intransitive harmonize with a melody; transitive harmonize a tune. Harmonise is common in British English.

### harmonize — Make compatible

To make different elements agree or work well together, or to fit well together.

làm hài hòa; phối hợp cho phù hợp

The clubs harmonized their event schedules to avoid clashes.

Các câu lạc bộ phối hợp lịch sự kiện của mình để tránh trùng giờ.

Transitive harmonize schedules; intransitive harmonize with something. Harmonise is a British spelling variant.

### 5. MANDATORY 22 FINDINGS

Resolve the following exact proposal keys.

#### A. 17 REVISE findings

1. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:a, an:indefinite`

   Correct the interpretation of the indefinite article. Do not use `a, an` as a canonical lemma without reviewing form/alias representation. Vietnamese meaning should reflect the grammatical article, not an unrelated object/noun interpretation.

2. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:abortion:end-pregnancy`

   Improve the learner-facing definition to distinguish the intended sense of deliberate termination of pregnancy. Preserve medical terminology uncertainty and do not overgeneralize.

3. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:about:approximately`

   Proposed POS correction: `PREPOSITION` → `ADVERB` for approximate number/quantity/time usage. Keep the separate topic-related preposition sense distinct. Confirm grammar before accepting the proposal.

4. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:able:competent`

   Improve the unnatural Vietnamese example translation while preserving the English meaning.

5. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:acquire:gain`

   Avoid a definition that incorrectly limits acquiring possessions to learning or experience. Decide whether to broaden the definition or record a semantic split proposal.

6. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:act:behave`

   Definition, sense label and example are not aligned. Choose between `behave` and `take action` based on the actual example and contextual evidence. Do not merge unrelated meanings.

7. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:action:movement`

   Align the proposed sense label with the actual example, which may express activity in progress rather than physical movement.

8. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:acute:perceptive`

   Replace unnatural collocation with a natural use, such as `acute sense of smell`, while keeping the definition semantically aligned.

9. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:age:grow-older`

   Improve the English example to demonstrate a natural use of `age` as a verb. Provide a faithful Vietnamese example translation.

10. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:arm:weapons`

    Preserve the distinction between body-part `arm` and plural `arms` meaning weapons. Propose plural-only usage notes and flag lemma/alias identity for owner review.

11. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:bad:spoiled-food`

    Use an example that clearly demonstrates food having spoiled rather than merely smelling unpleasant.

12. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:block:street-section`

    Align the sense definition with `two blocks from...`, distinguishing distance along city streets from the physical city block.

13. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:bread:baked-food`

    Improve definition specificity. Avoid defining bread as any food made by baking dough.

14. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:carbon dioxide:gas`

    Improve scientific accuracy: CO₂ molecules contain one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms. Keep learner-friendly wording.

15. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:coral reef:marine-formation`

16. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:cosmopolitan city:diverse-city`

17. `EDITORIAL_2H2B:fossil fuel:ancient-organic-fuel`

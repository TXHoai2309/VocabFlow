# Free practice and games do not reschedule vocabulary reviews

The owner chose free practice with separate results and no automatic change to
the scheduled review date. A user answering the same word ten times in a game
must not thereby advance it through ten scheduled review stages. This preserves
the distinction between extra repetition and scheduled recall, at the cost of
maintaining separate practice results. Existing exercise sessions currently use
the normal review/scheduler write, so their submission path must not be reused
unchanged for new free-practice or game results. Normal learning/review behavior
is not globally redefined by this decision. The owner subsequently accepted a
separate practice history (account sync; guests on their device) and no existing
XP/streak credit for the first practice release. The game interview subsequently
accepted private game points/records with no existing XP/streak credit in v1;
the point formula and game record rules remain open. Practice's product brief
was confirmed in Q14; implementation still has not been requested. This accepts
a product boundary only, not an implementation, schema or rollout.

Q43 extends the same separation to video watching/listening practice: its private
results do not automatically change vocabulary scheduling, XP, streak or learned
word counts. Watching to the end does not certify comprehension. Q44 confirmed
the complete video product contract; implementation has not been requested.
